Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Your Story, developed by GZapps, handles user data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Offline Gameplay: You can play local ("Quick Game" and "New Game") modes without creating an account. Saved stories and app preferences (language, theme) are stored locally on your device.
  • Online Multiplayer (Optional Account): If you choose to create an account to play Online Multiplayer, we collect your email address and authentication credentials via Google Firebase Authentication, and store your active game room and sentences in Google Cloud Firestore.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your email address and online room data are used solely to authenticate your account, display your username in multiplayer lobbies, and synchronize turns during online story games. We do not sell or share your personal data with third-party marketers.

3. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and associated profile data at any time directly inside the app by signing in on the Main Menu and tapping "Delete Account" (Διαγραφή Λογαριασμού), or by contacting us at gzappsdev@gmail.com.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services for authentication, multiplayer database, and advertising/billing, which are governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Privacy and Security

5. Children's Privacy

This game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy or wish to request data deletion, please contact us at:

Email: gzappsdev@gmail.com
